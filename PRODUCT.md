# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective members of Cali Terrain, a premium calisthenics and bodyweight-training gym in Secunderabad, Hyderabad. Mostly beginners-to-intermediates browsing on mobile after seeing the gym on Instagram or Maps. Their job: decide whether this gym is worth a trial visit. Their fear: "I'm not fit enough to start." Secondary audience: intermediate athletes evaluating coaching quality and facility seriousness.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site whose single conversion goal is a booked free trial session (routed through the global `onBookTrial` modal spine and WhatsApp). The site must make visitors imagine themselves progressing: first dead hang → first pull-up → muscle-up → levers. Success = trial bookings, not pageviews.

## Brand Personality

Premium sports-performance. Nike energy + Linear precision + Apple restraint. Three words: earned, cinematic, confident. The interface should feel like a progression ecosystem (paths, skill trees, unlocks, live feed of member firsts), never like a template gym site. Bold uppercase display type, dark facility-first photography, one electric blue accent.

## Anti-references

- Generic gym-template sites: stock photography, dumbbell clip art, red/black aggression, "JOIN NOW" pressure tactics.
- SaaS landing-page clichés: hero-metric cards, identical icon-card grids, gradient text.
- Crypto/gaming neon overload: the blue glow is an accent, not a rave.
- Anything that reads "wireframe" or unfinished: every media slot must feel cinematic even as a placeholder.

## Design Principles

1. **Story before content** — every section advances "where am I → where am I going → what unlocks next."
2. **One dominant element per section** — a path, a tree, a feed, a panorama. Never six equal cards.
3. **Vary density** — hero → sparse → dense → immersive → interactive → CTA. No grid-grid-grid.
4. **Reassurance scaffolding** — bold claims are always paired with "you don't have to be fit to start" micro-copy. Remove fear at every step.
5. **Photography is the surface** — text sits on imagery under dark scrims, not beside empty boxes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. `prefers-reduced-motion` is already honored across the animation layer and must stay honored in all new motion. Maintain contrast of secondary text (#9AA7B6) on dark surfaces; never drop copy below AA on scrims. Mobile-first: most visitors arrive from Instagram in-app browsers.
